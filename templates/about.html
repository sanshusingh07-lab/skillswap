Why SkillSwap exists

Learning works better when it goes both ways.

Students already have a surprising amount to share. SkillSwap makes it easier to find the right peer, start a useful conversation, and turn a small exchange into real progress.

Designed for the in-between moments.That hour between lectures. The project you keep putting off. The skill you wish a friend could explain.
The idea

A smaller, more human learning loop.

The platform is built around reciprocity, not passive consumption.
